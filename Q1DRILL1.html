MOBILE APP RECOMMENDATIONS

Here are the best mobile applications...


Google Docs

    Google Docs is a free, web-based word processor provided by Google. It lets you create, edit, and share text documents online in real time.

Zoom

    Zoom is a cloud communication platform for video meetings, team chat, voice calls, and virtual workspaces


Canva

    Canva is an online visual tool. It lets you make social media posts, presentations, posters, videos, and websites without professional design skills.
